Fintrackly – Free Personal Finance Tracker and Investment Portfolio Manager for India

Fintrackly helps Indian investors track their complete financial life in one private dashboard. Monitor net worth, stocks, mutual funds, SIPs, crypto, gold, silver, fixed deposits, PPF, NPS, expenses, liabilities and financial goals — all for free, with no broker access required. Import your portfolio from Zerodha, Groww, Angel One and INDmoney. Export as CSV or Excel. Sync to Notion.
